# 5-Day Generative AI Workshop
## From AI Beginner to AI Creator

**Target Audience:** B.Sc. Computer Science students  
**Duration:** 5 days × 4 scheduled hours = 20 hours, including a 15-minute break each day (18 hours 45 minutes of instruction)  
**Approach:** 20% Concepts + 60% Guided Hands-on + 20% Activities & Projects  
**Prerequisites:** Basic computer and internet skills. Prior programming is helpful but not required; coding extensions are optional.

---

## 📋 Workshop Overview

This workshop empowers students with practical Generative AI skills through interactive demonstrations, hands-on activities, and guided projects. Students will learn to use AI tools effectively and create practical AI-assisted study and career resources.

### Learning Outcomes

By the end of this workshop, students will:
- ✅ Understand AI fundamentals and how Generative AI works
- ✅ Create effective prompts and use AI productively
- ✅ Create and critically evaluate a document-grounded AI study assistant
- ✅ Explain how prompts, models, context, retrieval, and AI workflows fit together
- ✅ Use AI responsibly for coding, debugging, testing, and technical learning
- ✅ Complete two practical AI projects
- ✅ Understand career opportunities in AI

---

## 📅 5-Day Curriculum

| Day | Topic | Main Outcome |
|-----|-------|--------------|
| **Day 1** | AI & Generative AI Foundations | Explain core concepts, limitations, and CS use cases |
| **Day 2** | Prompting & AI for Computer Science | Use AI to explain, debug, document, and test code responsibly |
| **Day 3** | Document-Grounded AI | Project 1: CS Course Knowledge Assistant |
| **Day 4** | AI Workflows and Agents | Design a workflow; optional guided automation challenge |
| **Day 5** | Final Project & Careers | Project 2: CS Career and Technical Interview Assistant |

## 🎯 Key Features

### Beginner-Friendly, CS-Relevant Approach
- Uses accessible AI tools and explains the technical ideas behind them
- No programming prerequisite; optional Python/Colab extensions for students who are ready
- Connects AI literacy to software development, data, testing, and security

### Student-Centered Learning
- Real-world problems relevant to students
- Peer learning and collaboration
- Hands-on demonstrations and guided practice

### Career Focus
- Resume building and interview preparation
- Career exploration and skill development
- Understanding AI career opportunities

### Practical Projects
- **Project 1:** CS Course Knowledge Assistant in a document-grounded workspace; this is not a standalone chatbot application
- **Project 2:** Reusable CS Career and Technical Interview Assistant, designed and tested with a structured prompt

## 👨‍🏫 Facilitation Notes

1. **Classroom Setup:** Ensure students have devices and internet, with an offline fallback
2. **Pre-Workshop:** Test institution-approved tools; do not require personal or paid accounts
3. **Pacing:** Allow flexibility for different learning speeds
4. **Support:** Provide step-by-step guides for tool access and usage
5. **Engagement:** Encourage experimentation and creative applications

1. **For Instructors:**
   - Review the daily teaching guides and activity files

---

## ✨ Key Principles

- **Concepts to Practice:** Explain technical ideas clearly, then apply them
- **Learn by Doing:** Maximum hands-on practice over lectures
- **Immediate Application:** Focus on skills students can use right away
- **Creative Freedom:** Encourage experimentation and personalization
- **Inclusivity:** Do not assume prior AI, Python, API, or database experience
